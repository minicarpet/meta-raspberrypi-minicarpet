### Minicarpet build environment ready. ###

Build the SD-card image, ext4 rootfs and SWUpdate bundle with:

    bitbake minicarpet-image

Expected deploy artifacts include:

    minicarpet-image-minicarpet-rpi4.rootfs.ext4
    minicarpet-image-minicarpet-rpi4.rootfs.wic.bz2
    minicarpet-image-minicarpet-rpi4.rootfs.swu

Useful checks:

    bitbake-layers show-appends
    bitbake-getvar IMAGE_FSTYPES
    bitbake-getvar WKS_FILE
    bitbake-getvar CMDLINE_ROOTFS
